🌠 Celestial Bodies (Complete Guide)

⭐ Stars

☀️
The Sun
Our star at the center of the Solar System. Mass: 1.989 × 10³⁰ kg. Surface temp: 5,500°C.
📹 Kurzgesagt
✨
Sirius (Dog Star)
Brightest star in Earth's night sky. Distance: 8.6 light-years. Part of Canis Major constellation.
📹 Vsauce
🔴
Betelgeuse
Red supergiant star. 700 times larger than the Sun. Distance: 640 light-years away.
📹 Crash Course

🌙 Moons

🌙
Earth's Moon
Natural satellite of Earth. Diameter: 3,474 km. Causes tides and stabilizes Earth's tilt.
📹 Kurzgesagt
🪐
Ganymede
Largest moon in Solar System. Moon of Jupiter. Diameter: 5,268 km. Has icy surface.
📹 Veritasium
🔵
Titan
Moon of Saturn. Has thick atmosphere. Only moon with liquid on surface (methane lakes).
📹 PBS Space Time

☄️ Asteroids & Comets

🪨
Ceres
Dwarf planet in asteroid belt. Diameter: 946 km. Contains 1/3 of asteroid belt's mass.
📹 Crash Course
☄️
Halley's Comet
Famous periodic comet. Returns every 75-76 years. Last seen 1986, next 2061.
📹 Kurzgesagt
💫
Bennu Asteroid
Near-Earth asteroid. Diameter: 492 meters. Visited by OSIRIS-REx spacecraft. Risk of impact in 2182.
📹 Veritasium

🌍 Exoplanets

🪐
Proxima Centauri b
Closest exoplanet to Earth. Distance: 4.24 light-years. Earth-sized, in habitable zone.
📹 Crash Course
🔥
WASP-12b
Ultra-hot Jupiter. Extreme temperatures (1,600°C). Orbits very close to its star.
📹 PBS Space Time
🌿
TRAPPIST-1e
Earth-sized exoplanet. In habitable zone of TRAPPIST-1 star. Distance: 39 light-years.
📹 Kurzgesagt
