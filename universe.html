🌍 Local Group

The cluster of galaxies containing our Milky Way, Andromeda, and Triangulum. About 80 galaxies in total, spanning 10 million light-years.

🌌 Observable Universe

The portion of the universe we can observe from Earth. The farthest objects are about 46.5 billion light-years away due to cosmic expansion.

⭐ Key Objects

Milky Way: Our home galaxy with 100-400 billion stars. Andromeda: Nearest large galaxy, 2.5 million light-years away.

🚀 Scale Comparison

If the observable universe were the size of Earth, a galaxy would be smaller than a grain of sand, and a star would be invisible to the naked eye!

🌟 Light-Year

Distance light travels in one year: ~9.46 trillion kilometers. Seeing distant galaxies means looking back in time - Andromeda light took 2.5 million years to reach us!

🔭 Hubble Volume

The sphere-shaped region around Earth that we can observe. Beyond this cosmic horizon, light hasn't had time to reach us since the Big Bang.
